# Claude Certified Architect (CCA-F): unofficial prep kit

Files from the Zero Cost AI video **"Claude Certified Architect Prep: Unofficial Kit (Not by Anthropic)"**.

> **Not official. Not provided by Anthropic.** These files are independent study material. They are not made, provided, reviewed or endorsed by Anthropic, and they are not affiliated with Anthropic. The questions are original practice questions, not real exam questions. Exam details can change: always confirm them on the official exam page before you book.

## The 3 files

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| [`Claude_Certified_Architect_Foundations_Study_Guide.pdf`](example-files/Claude_Certified_Architect_Foundations_Study_Guide.pdf) | 57-page study guide: every exam domain explained for beginners, with code, diagrams, labs and quizzes |
| [`CCA-F_Mock_Exams.pdf`](example-files/CCA-F_Mock_Exams.pdf) | 96-page mock exam workbook: 10 exams, 200 questions, answer sheets and full answer keys |
| [`CCA-F_Mock_Exams.exe`](example-files/CCA-F_Mock_Exams.exe) | Windows practice app: the same 10 exams in practice and timed modes, score by domain, review of misses |

To download a file, open it above and click **Download raw file** (the download icon at the top right).

## How to use them

1. Read the study guide one domain at a time, and do the lab at the end of each chapter.
2. Run `CCA-F_Mock_Exams.exe` and choose **Start the practice app**. Take exams 1 to 5 in **Practice** mode and read every explanation.
3. Take exams 6 to 10 in **Timed** mode (40 minutes each, no notes).
4. Review your misses. Aim for 16/20 or better before booking the real exam.

- The app is a single file: no install, works offline. Windows only.
- Windows SmartScreen may warn you the first time because the file isn't signed: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- Prefer paper? Print `CCA-F_Mock_Exams.pdf`; each exam has an answer sheet and an answer key.
- The estimated scaled score in the app is a simple linear estimate, not the official scoring formula.
