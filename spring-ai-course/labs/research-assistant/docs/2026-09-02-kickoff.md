# Checkout service: project kickoff (2026-09-02)

Attendees: Lena (tech lead), Sam (platform), Priya (product), Omar (QA)

## Goal
Rebuild the Brightwave checkout service so that a product page and the cart load fast,
even during the November sales week.

## Current state
- The catalog API answers in 840 ms at p95. Most of that time is spent loading prices and stock.
- Every checkout request reads the same product data again from PostgreSQL.
- The service runs on 4 instances behind the load balancer. In the sales week we scale to 12.

## Targets
- p95 latency of the catalog API under 300 ms.
- Zero wrong prices shown to customers.
- No new on-call pages during the sales week.

## Scope
In scope: catalog reads, cart, price display.
Out of scope: payments (handled by the payments team), the mobile app.

## Ideas discussed
- Add a cache in front of the catalog reads. Options to compare: a local in-memory cache
  per instance, or a shared cache. Lena will write an ADR.
- Pre-compute the "featured products" list every 5 minutes.
- Move images to the CDN (already planned by the web team, not our project).
