# Adult IQ Test: free Windows app

Files from the Zero Cost AI video **"Free IQ Test App for Adults: How Sharp Is Your Mind?"**.

A timed IQ-style test for adults: 32 questions across numerical, verbal, logical and pattern/spatial
reasoning, a 25-minute timer, and instant results with an estimated IQ, your percentile, a score per
skill area and an answer review that explains every question.

> The score is a fun estimate for self-assessment, not a clinical IQ test.

## Download

**[Download IQTest-1.0.0-windows.zip](https://github.com/NEDBS12/youtube-tuto/raw/main/iq-test-promo/download/IQTest-1.0.0-windows.zip)** (Windows 10/11, about 75 MB)

1. Unzip the file anywhere on your PC (for example your Desktop).
2. Open the `IQTest` folder and double-click **IQTest.exe**.
3. A small control window opens and the test appears in your browser. Close the window (or click **Exit**) to stop the app.

No installation and no Java needed: the folder includes everything it needs. Keep the files together;
`IQTest.exe` won't start if you move it out of its folder.

Windows may show a "Windows protected your PC" message because the app isn't signed. Click
**More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## Tips for your best score

- Find a quiet spot and give yourself 25 uninterrupted minutes.
- Don't rush: the hardest questions are worth three times as much.
- Stuck? Skip it and come back with the question map.
- Afterwards, read the explanations in the answer review.
